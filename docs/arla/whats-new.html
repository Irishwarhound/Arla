Release notes

What's new in Arla

Updates come with your licence (version 1.x is included). Arla tells you when one is available and never installs without your say-so.

1.1.4 · October 20, 2026

  • Review forms beside the chat: when Arla opens a plan, goal, task, routine or check for you to review, the form now opens next to the chat instead of covering it. In a narrow window, Back to chat switches between the two.
  • Plans keep your times: "movie at 10:15, game at 11:30" now keeps those times instead of moving them.
  • Fixes: a persona is asked only when the Brain decides you meant that persona; a goal the Brain sends incomplete gets corrected instead of failing; Research's review moves on to the next free reviewer when one doesn't answer in time.

1.1.3 · October 19, 2026

  • Sticky notes per project: each project keeps its own sticky notes, and switching projects shows that project's notes. You can move a note to another project.
  • Your personas on the phone: a linked phone now shows the personas you made on the computer and can edit, add and delete them; changes go to the computer when it's back.
  • Fixes: the assistant's name in the title bar is no longer cut off; the Tools page's guide is now called "Add a tool server (MCP)".
  • Details in the privacy policy (version 2.2).

1.1.2 · October 19, 2026

  • More free Brain models: Cloudflare's free chat models now qualify as Brains, and so do Mistral's (a "slow down" reply is now a pause, not a failed check).
  • Connect a service: NVIDIA NIM, xAI, Ollama Cloud, Hugging Face and DeepSeek can be connected right from Connect a service.
  • Fixes: Send keys to this phone opens its form under the phone's row; the free or paid models question uses Arla's own pop-up; the paid-model choices on the Models page are readable in every theme; Cloud models and your keys can be collapsed.

1.1.1 · October 18, 2026

  • Deep research (new): in Research, choose Deep for a question that needs more than one search. Arla plans the question, then works in up to four steps: it searches, reads the pages, writes notes on what it found, what's missing and where sources disagree, and plans the next step from those notes. Then it writes a cited report and has it reviewed: each main finding is checked against quotes from the pages, and every source is rated. A progress view shows each step as it runs, and the saved report keeps how it was researched. When a service is busy or out of its allowance for the day, Deep moves on to the next one you've connected.
  • What your AI use costs: the Work Log shows today's AI cost, and Impact shows your real cloud costs from the token counts each service reports and its published prices. Calls without a published price are shown as "not priced", never guessed. Opening usage details can read your remaining balance from OpenRouter or DeepSeek with your own key.
  • Fixes: read-aloud with Cloudflare's voice works again; Cloudflare speech fills in your saved account; the Recent changes button is gone from the main chat.
  • Details in the privacy policy (version 2.1).

1.1.0 · October 17, 2026

  • Connect many more services: about 20 more AI services are ready to connect with your own account (SambaNova, Together, Fireworks, DeepSeek, Cerebras and more), your own custom AI address now works on the phone too, and Windows adds AWS Bedrock. A service that may train on what you send asks you first.
  • Voice: cloud transcription for meetings and dictation (Deepgram, AssemblyAI, Cloudflare and more) and natural read-aloud voices (ElevenLabs, Cloudflare and more). Speech on your device stays the default.
  • Images: fal, Replicate and seven more image services in Image Studio.
  • Research and memory: more search sources (Brave, SerpApi, SearchApi or your own SearXNG), a real browser for pages that used to fail, and memory search without a local model (Jina, Cloudflare, Pinecone, Voyage).
  • Text from photos and offline translation (Android): photos in your Inbox get their text read on the phone, and you can long-press any text and choose Translate offline.
  • Side-button assistant (Android): hold the side key for a small glass assistant over any app. Share screen sends a picture of what you were looking at with one message.
  • Tools: Wolfram Alpha and DeepL as tools, MCP servers on the phone, and Qwen Code as a fourth assistant that codes (Windows).
  • Goals: reopen a finished goal; pausing or ending a routine shows what stays.
  • Smaller fixes: "Add as a task" on offline notes, scheduled emails on the phone can turn on exact alarms, checks and automations made on the phone move to your computer, the floating name no longer covers the chat, and models that read pictures are tagged.
  • Details in the privacy policy (version 2.0).

1.0.9 · October 16, 2026

  • Scheduled emails on both devices: with your phone linked and the same email account connected on both, a scheduled email has a backup: if your computer is off at send time, the phone sends it about ten minutes later instead. It is sent once, never twice, and a late email asks you before it goes.
  • Your personas, by context: Arla tells your persona apart from another app or the ordinary word with the same name (your persona Muse, Muse Code, or a muse) and shows "Asked Muse (your persona)" when it hands over. Persona names need at least 3 characters.
  • Sticky notes: binder tabs on Home, and any color from a color wheel (yellow by default).
  • Smaller fixes: a stopped timer shows 0:00; goal lengths count the start day; the phone says plainly when your computer didn't finish a reply; "color" is spelled the US way everywhere.
  • Details in the privacy policy (version 1.9).

1.0.8 · October 14, 2026

  • Assistants that code (Windows): hand a coding task to Claude Code, GitHub Copilot or Muse Code from the Tools page. Arla starts that company's own program, signed in with your own account and plan, and shows its answer and which files changed. Arla never sees or stores your sign-in for them.
  • Chat always answers: when none of your own AI services or your linked computer can answer, Arla uses free public models for plain chat (on by default; you can turn them off in Workshop), then a model on your device.
  • Offline chat on the phone: the phone app sets up a small offline chat model the first time (on Wi-Fi unless you choose mobile data), so simple chat works without internet. Plans, email and other actions need one of your AI services.
  • Details in the privacy policy (version 1.8).

1.0.7 · October 13, 2026

  • Goals with plans and routines: a goal can hold a dated plan or an ongoing routine, with its daily tasks and checks along the way. A check can suggest changes, which you see in a form before anything changes.
  • Research in the background: the Research assistant searches, reads the sources, puts the most reliable ones first and lists pages it couldn't read instead of citing them.
  • Ask about your computer: its hardware, disks, programs, a folder's size or a file's text, looked up with read-only tools. Reading outside Arla's workspace asks you first.
  • Arla can change files for you (Windows): new files and edits happen straight away with Undo for 30 days; Arla asks once before it overwrites, renames, moves or deletes anything, and deleted files go to the Recycle Bin. Only models that pass Arla's file-writing test do this, including the free GLM-5.3 through NVIDIA.
  • Email and calendar (early access) on the computer and the phone: Arla drafts, sends, schedules and adds events only after you confirm in a form.
  • Sync away from home (early access, off by default): your phone and computer can reach each other through an encrypted relay when they aren't on the same network.
  • Arla picks the most capable AI model that works, the same way on the computer and the phone; a paid model is used only after you agree.
  • Details in the terms (version 1.3) and the privacy policy (version 1.7).

1.0.6 · October 5, 2026

  • Inbox sorts what you drop: things you add wait a few seconds in the box, then Arla files them, with a short note when they're added and sorted. Tags, and previews for pictures.
  • Timer and Stopwatch panels on Home, on the computer and the phone.
  • Routines and checks on every timeline and in Today, and Today's pop-out is the same panel as on Home.
  • Image Studio and Google Images panels open in their own window.
  • Your phone app is included: a linked phone can ask this computer whether Arla is licensed (only yes or no, never your key), so the phone app needs no separate purchase. Details in the terms (version 1.2, Arla asks you to accept once) and the privacy policy.

1.0.5 · October 3, 2026

  • Deleting a project asks first and lists what will go (tasks, goals, plans, routines, notes, memories and more). Arla saves a backup, then removes everything it kept for that project; your own project folder is never touched. Phones can delete projects too.
  • Send a problem report in one click: Help → Report a problem now has Send to Irishwarhound. You still read the whole report first, and nothing is sent until you press Send. Details in the privacy policy.
  • The Arla phone app works on its own: without a computer it keeps its own tasks, plans and memory; when you link Arla for Windows, you choose when to bring them over.
  • Forms close when you're done, and the licence key form folds away once Arla is activated.

1.0.4 · October 2, 2026

  • Leave a review from Help: it opens the review page on irishwarhound.com.
  • Anonymous trial reports: while a copy is in its free trial, it tells us the trial is running, at most once a day, with a random ID made only for this and nothing about you or your PC. It helps us spot trials that run longer than they should. Details in the privacy policy.

1.0.3 · October 2, 2026

  • Signed by Cody Dean: the installer and app are code-signed, so Windows shows who made them.
  • Large files location: move offline models, voices, downloads, backups and saved pictures to another drive in one step (Settings → Storage). Arla checks every copy before switching and tells you if that drive is unplugged later.
  • New looks: Rose Glass, Forest, Lavender and Sandstone themes, and your own background picture.
  • Help and Report a problem: getting started, what's new, and a problem report you read before sharing.
  • Fixes from testing on another PC: no more console windows flashing during setup; research answers and tables display properly; error messages stay until you dismiss them; the speaker button reports what went wrong instead of staying silent; one failed starter-pack item no longer stops the rest; a smoother idle with Eco on; collapsible Models lists with an Unload button; and the tour points at the right buttons.

1.0.2 · October 1, 2026

  • Starter packs: one click installs Ollama, offline models and voice, sized to your PC (Base, Standard, Power), with progress, cancel and resume.
  • A guided tour on first start: your name, the starter pack, connecting a free cloud AI, and a walk through every part of the app.
  • Conversation history is kept until you delete it, in Knowledge → Memory → Conversation history, with clear labels on what Arla learned from your chats.
  • Safer restores and phone sync: an interrupted restore always finishes cleanly, and an out-of-date phone can't overwrite newer edits.
  • Clearer behaviour: sleep, lock and shutdown failures are reported; unsent messages survive a restart; milestones ask before being marked done from guesswork; dates stay right across daylight-saving changes.
  • New installs use the encrypted phone link only.

1.0.0 · October 1, 2026

  • The first release: one assistant and one conversation across your Windows PC and Android phone, with projects, goals, plans, Today, Inbox, memory, research, routines and more.
  • A 3-day free trial, then a one-time licence for 2 computers.
