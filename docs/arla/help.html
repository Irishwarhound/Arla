Getting started with Arla

Everything you need for the first hour, in order. If you get stuck, email support@irishwarhound.com.

Will Arla run on my PC?

WindowsWindows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
Memory8 GB minimum. 16 GB or more is recommended for Arla's offline models (the Standard pack); 32 GB for the Power pack.
Free disk spaceAbout 10 GB for the Base starter pack, 25 GB for Standard, 35 GB for Power. You can keep the large files on another drive (Settings → Storage).
Graphics cardOptional. A graphics card with 6 GB or more of its own memory makes offline answers much faster.
InternetTo download Arla and the starter pack, to activate a licence (once, then a quick check about once a month), and for cloud AI services if you connect them. Offline models work without internet.
Phone (optional)An Android phone for the companion app (coming to the Play Store): included with your licence, or $5.99 once on its own after a 7-day free trial.

1. Install

  1. Download Arla for Windows with the link in your beta welcome email, and run the installer. (Arla is in beta: the public download opens with buying, after the beta.)
  2. If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box, choose More info, then Run anyway. The installer is signed by Cody Dean (Windows shows the publisher there), but Windows can still show this box for new apps while they build a reputation; it doesn't mean anything is wrong.
  3. Arla opens and asks you to accept the terms.

Beta testers: enter your beta key (step 4) whenever you like. Without a key, the first 3 days are a free trial with everything switched on. No card or payment details are needed, and nothing is ever charged automatically.

2. The tour and the starter pack

A short tour starts by itself. It asks your name, then offers a starter pack: the free programs and models that let Arla work on your computer without the internet (Ollama, a few offline AI models, and voice). Arla suggests a pack that suits your PC and shows the download size first. Choose Base if you're unsure; you can add more later in Workshop → Models.

The download continues in the background while you finish the tour. Windows may ask once to allow Ollama to install.

3. Connect a free cloud AI (recommended)

Cloud AI services make Arla faster and smarter, and several are free. The tour walks you through it, or later open Settings → AI services → Connect a cloud AI, step by step. Good free choices are Groq, Google Gemini and OpenRouter; connecting two or three means Arla always has one available. You paste a key from the service's website; it's stored encrypted on your PC.

4. Enter your licence key

After buying, the page you land on shows your key (like ARLA-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). In Arla, open Settings → Licence, paste the key and choose Activate. One licence covers 2 computers; to move it, choose Deactivate this computer on the old one first.

After the trial, without a key, Arla becomes read-only: you can still open, search, export and back up everything. Nothing is ever locked or deleted.

5. Keep large files on another drive

Offline models are large. To keep them, the voice models, the download cache, backups and saved pictures on another drive (for example an external one), open Settings → Storage, choose a folder there, and Arla moves everything for you, checking each copy before switching. If that drive is unplugged later, Arla still opens and tells you; cloud AI keeps working.

6. Getting help

  • Something isn't working: in Arla, open Help → Report a problem. It prepares a report you can read first; nothing is sent automatically. Bugs go to beta@irishwarhound.com.
  • Questions, your licence or a refund: support@irishwarhound.com. Refunds within 7 days of buying, in full.
  • Lost your key or your old computer? Use the Lost your key? page with the address you bought with: we'll email your key and a link to free the old computer's place. Stuck? Email support from that address.
  • See the tour again: Help → Take the tour again.
  • Tell others what you think: Help → Leave a review, or write a review here.
