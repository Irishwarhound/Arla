Accessibility

Updated November 13, 2026

Irishwarhound LLC wants everyone to be able to use Arla and this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

What we do

  • This website is checked with the axe accessibility checker. On November 13, 2026 every page passed its WCAG 2.1 A and AA checks, and we checked text colour contrast by hand where the checker could not decide.
  • The Arla apps label their controls for screen readers, let you reach most controls from the keyboard on Windows, and have light and dark themes. Our automated tests check layout and text contrast on many screens before each release.
  • We don't use accessibility "overlay" widgets. We fix the pages and the apps themselves.

Known limits

Some parts of Arla are new or change often, and some content comes from AI services and from files you import, which we don't control. If something gets in your way, we want to hear about it.

Tell us about a problem

Email support@irishwarhound.com with "Accessibility" in the subject. Say which page or screen you were on, what you were trying to do, and which assistive technology you use, if any. We'll reply, work on a fix, and help you get what you need another way in the meantime.
