# Arla Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0 · Effective September 17, 2026**

_Have a licensed Idaho attorney review this before you sell Arla. It was drafted with AI help and is not legal advice._

IrishwarhoundGaming ("we", "us") makes Arla, a personal AI hub for Windows and Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

**The short version: we don't collect anything.** Arla has no accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics and no tracking. Your data stays on your own devices unless you choose to send it to an AI service or another app.

---

## 1. What we collect
**Nothing.** We operate no servers that receive your data, and Arla does not report usage, crashes, device identifiers or any other information to us. There is no account to create and no sign-in with us.

If you email us for support, we receive whatever you choose to put in that email.

## 2. What Arla stores on your devices
All of this stays on the computer or phone where it was created:
- your chats with Arla and with any model, and the summaries made from them;
- memories, notes, goals, milestones, plans, inbox items and work log entries;
- documents you add to the Library, and the search index built from them;
- settings, personas, skills, home layout and labels;
- photos you capture into the inbox (stored in the app's own storage, not your gallery);
- chat history you import from other apps;
- a small local problem log of errors, so you can see what went wrong;
- API keys you add, stored with the operating system's own protection (Windows DPAPI through Electron safeStorage; Android Keystore through Expo SecureStore).

You can view, edit, export and delete all of it in the app, or by deleting the app's data folder.

## 3. What leaves your device, and only when you choose it
1. **AI services you connect.** If you send a request to a cloud model (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral or NVIDIA), the content of that request goes to that provider: your message, plus whatever context Arla includes, such as relevant notes, memories, the current page or a file you attached. Their privacy policy and terms then apply. Models running on your own device send nothing.
2. **Model downloads.** Downloading a model contacts that model's host (for example Ollama or Hugging Face).
3. **Your own devices.** Pairing a phone with your computer sends data between them over your own network or your own private network (such as Tailscale). It does not pass through us.
4. **Web pages you ask for.** If you ask Arla to read a page, it contacts that site.
5. **Backups you make.** Backups go where you put them: a folder you choose, your own computer, or a cloud drive you select.

Arla never sends your data to us, and never sells or shares it with anyone.

## 4. Children
Arla is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Since we collect nothing at all, there is nothing for us to delete; removing the app removes its data from that device.

## 5. Permissions the apps ask for
- **Camera (phone):** only when you tap the camera button to capture a photo into your inbox.
- **Microphone:** only while you use voice input.
- **Files and folders:** to read documents you add to the Library, to save backups where you choose, and to write files you ask for.
- **Network:** to reach the AI services and models you choose, and your own paired devices.
- **Notifications:** for reminders and scheduled items you create.

You can refuse any of these; the related feature simply won't run.

## 6. Your rights
Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. You control everything directly in the app: export it, edit it or delete it at any time. If a cloud AI provider holds a copy of what you sent them, contact that provider under their policy.

## 7. Security
Data stays on your devices, protected by your device's own security. Keys are stored with the operating system's protected storage and are never shown in chat or sent to your phone. You are responsible for your device passwords, your network, and who you pair with. No system is perfectly secure.

## 8. Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page with a new date.
