Arla

One place for all your AI. It runs on your own computer, works from your phone, and learns how you work.

Windows · Android (soon)Early preview · v0.1.0Private by default

Arla brings together the models on your machine, free cloud services and any paid accounts you have. Tell her what you want in plain words: she picks the right mind for the job, brings in specialists when a task needs a team, and makes the change for you. The goal is simple — less money spent, less time lost, less attention wasted, and less of your potential sitting unused in tools that forget you between sessions.

Download for Windows

Windows may warn about an unknown publisher until the installer is signed. Android version coming to the Play Store.

What it does

Say what you want, plainly, and Arla does it — changes settings, opens pages, rearranges Home, files your Inbox, opens a terminal, or puts the machine to sleep on a schedule. Talk over her mid-reply and a correction folds into what she's already doing instead of making you wait.

Minds — every way to talk to a model

  • Brain — the hub-wide assistant. Auto-routes to the right model, or pin one yourself.
  • Personas — Code Buddy, Muse, Critic, Scout and more, each with its own voice; make your own.
  • Deck — several chat slots side by side so you can compare models instead of guessing.
  • Chat — one plain conversation with a model you choose.
  • Relay — chain models into a pipeline, each step fed the last one's output.

Plan — where the work gets tracked

  • Inbox — capture now, sort later.
  • Timeline — milestones paced to your deadline, marked done only from real evidence.
  • Projects & Goals — every project keeps its own goals and milestones.
  • Work log and Routines — a running record, and jobs that run on a schedule.

Knowledge — memory and your own documents

  • Memory — ranked, and reorganizes itself on its own.
  • Library — your notes, PDFs and code, searchable by every model; where an imported ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot export ends up.

Workshop — how Arla stays capable

  • Tools — connect external MCP servers, start and stop them, approve what they touch.
  • Models — manage local Ollama models and connected cloud providers.
  • Skills — small playbooks Arla writes herself; each has to pass its own tests to be saved.
  • Specialists — the team she can pull in for a job big enough to earn one.
  • Checks — the same build/syntax/page checks that gate a real release, visible to you.

Bring your own model

Fully offline with Ollama, or connect OpenRouter, Groq, LM Studio, OpenAI, Anthropic, or your own self-hosted endpoint — mix them freely, with a usage meter for anything metered.

Make it yours, and take it with you

Five themes — glass, midnight, aurora, sunset, daylight — a Home built from the panels you pick, and an Android companion that pairs to the same hub over your own network.

Private by default. No account, no servers of ours, no tracking.

What you need
Windows 10 or 11. For models on your own computer, install Ollama (free). No graphics card required: Arla sizes models to the machine it finds.
