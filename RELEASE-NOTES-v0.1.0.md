# Arla 0.1.0

The first public build of Arla for Windows.

## In this release
- **Arla, your assistant.** Ask in plain words: she changes settings, opens pages, edits projects and personas, arranges Home, manages goals and your inbox, and runs things for you.
- **Request scoring.** Every message is measured for how much reasoning it needs and what it costs to get wrong, then handled by the smallest model that can do it well: local models first, a free cloud model for the hard ones, and a team of specialists for big jobs.
- **Timeline.** Set a deadline and Arla paces the remaining milestones to it and to how you actually work, marks milestones your work has finished (with an undo on every one), warns when a goal slips, and offers to take on what she can.
- **Skills.** Small pre-checked guides and tested calculations for common jobs. Arla writes new ones as she learns, and each must pass its own tests before it is saved.
- **Talk over her.** Keep typing while she works: corrections and added thoughts fold into the running request, new questions queue, and small ones are answered at once on a local model.
- **Knowledge.** Memory that tidies itself, a searchable Library of your own documents, and chat history imported from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot.
- **Your computer.** Sleep, lock, restart or wake it on a schedule, set reminders, and open a terminal in a glass window inside the app.
- **Phone companion.** Pair an Android phone to use your own models, projects and goals anywhere, with a cloud or on-device stand-in when the computer is out of reach.
- **Private by default.** No account, no servers of ours, no tracking.

## Notes
- The installer is not signed yet, so Windows shows an "unknown publisher" warning. Choose More info → Run anyway.
- Voice models are downloaded on first use rather than bundled, to keep the installer small.
- Local models need [Ollama](https://ollama.com). Without it, connect a free cloud service in Settings.
